TASK SKILL: PROPORTIONAL PLANNING

Use for broad tasks, decisions, and multi-step plans.
1. Define the desired outcome and constraints, then choose the fewest steps that achieve it.
2. Separate reversible experiments from irreversible actions; request confirmation before consequential external changes.
3. Give each step an observable completion condition and a recovery path for likely failures.
4. Do not add agents, tools, services, or dependencies unless they provide a concrete advantage.
5. Finish with a result and evidence, not merely a claim that the plan should work.
