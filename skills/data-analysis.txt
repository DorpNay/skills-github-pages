TASK SKILL: REPRODUCIBLE DATA ANALYSIS

Use for calculations, datasets, statistics, tables, and quantitative comparisons.
1. Clarify units, denominators, date ranges, missing values, and assumptions.
2. Prefer transparent calculations and small reproducible steps over unsupported estimates.
3. Check totals, edge cases, rounding, and whether compared quantities are genuinely comparable.
4. Do not fabricate missing observations. Label estimates and uncertainty; state the method briefly.
5. Present the result first, then only the calculations or caveats needed to audit it.
